# Plotline release notes

Every change to the site, newest first. Also published at https://djwalsted.github.io/Plotline/#release-notes

## v0.11.0 · 2026-11-09 · Comment review

- **New:** Comments are reviewed before they are published, to keep names and personal details off the site.
- **Improved:** Reviewers can remove a name from a comment and publish the rest.
- **Improved:** The survey and results pages explain that comments appear after review.

## v0.10.0 · 2026-11-08 · How did you hear about Plotline?

- **New:** An optional question on your first survey: how you heard about Plotline. It is asked once and never shown publicly.
- **Improved:** Shared links, QR codes and flyers fill in the answer for you, so we can tell which ways of reaching neighbors work best.

## v0.9.0 · 2026-11-08 · Share kit

- **New:** Share button on every site and results page, with a copy-ready link.
- **New:** QR code for every site and area, ready to download for posts, flyers and signs.
- **New:** Printable flyer for every site, sized for letter paper, for storefront windows, coffee shop boards and mailboxes.
- **New:** Share this area button on each area page.

## v0.8.0 · 2026-11-07 · Release notes

- **New:** This page. Every change to Plotline is listed here, newest first.
- **New:** The current version number is shown in the footer.

## v0.7.0 · 2026-11-07 · Areas across Timnath and Windsor

- **New:** Weigh in now opens a map of areas: Old Town Timnath, I-25 & Harmony, Downtown Windsor, and I-25 & Highway 392.
- **New:** Seven new sites with suggested ideas, including a grocery store, a gas station and market, a drive-thru restaurant, a family restaurant, an ice cream shop, a mixed-use district and a hotel.
- **New:** Each area has its own page, map and lettered sites.
- **Improved:** Each survey now asks how far you live from that site, instead of from Timnath’s Main Street.
- **New:** New results filter: within 2 miles.
- **New:** New answer options: fast food / drive-thru, park / green space, homes / apartments, and hotel.
- **New:** The developer page explains how to survey a whole area, like a portfolio of sites.

## v0.6.1 · 2026-11-07 · Clearer navigation

- **Improved:** The main menu now reads Weigh in · Get notified · About.
- **Improved:** The list of sites is titled “Open for feedback.”

## v0.6.0 · 2026-11-07 · Privacy policy

- **New:** Alpha privacy policy, under attorney review.
- **New:** Site footer with links to privacy, About, notifications and survey requests.
- **New:** A privacy policy link next to every form that collects information.
- **Improved:** Site feedback is no longer linked to your survey answers.

## v0.5.2 · 2026-11-07 · Separate sign-up list

- **Improved:** Notification sign-ups are stored in their own list, separate from survey answers.

## v0.5.1 · 2026-11-07 · Our promise

- **New:** “We sell what the neighborhood thinks. Never who you are.” Shown on the About page, the home page, every survey, the sign-up page and the developer page.

## v0.5.0 · 2026-11-07 · Get notified and Plotline On Demand

- **New:** Get notified: sign up by email or text to hear about new projects near you.
- **New:** Plotline On Demand: a page for developers, owners and cities to request a survey, with reach options from 200 to 5,000+ residents.

## v0.4.0 · 2026-11-07 · About Plotline

- **New:** About page explaining why residents and developers use Plotline.
- **New:** Answer options for a full grocery store and a gas station / convenience store.

## v0.3.1 · 2026-11-07 · Lettered sites

- **Improved:** Sites are lettered A, B, C… on the map, the legend, the cards and each survey, so they are easy to match up.

## v0.3.0 · 2026-11-06 · Map

- **New:** Map of downtown Timnath with a pin for each site, a 10-minute walking circle and the Poudre River Trail.
- **Improved:** The idea for 4138 Main St changed to a kitchen and patio, since a brewery already operates in the building.

## v0.2.0 · 2026-11-06 · Suggested ideas

- **New:** Each site shows a suggested idea and an illustration of what could go there.

## v0.1.0 · 2026-11-06 · First test version

- **New:** Surveys for four Main Street buildings in Old Town Timnath: what you want there, a rating of one idea, and how you would use it.
- **New:** Public results with filters for walking distance, town, trail riders, weekend mornings and families.
- **New:** Answers are saved and shared live.
